Where the work is

Darker green means more strong matches. A strong match is a home whose public records point to siding that is likely due. Grey towns are taken: one crew per territory.

What's in each town

Every home we checked, by how good a match it is for siding work. Towns with the most strong matches are on top.

How few make the cut

We don't mail everyone. A home has to score 0.85 or higher out of 1 to count as a strong match.

The numbers come from public town records, the Connecticut state parcel layer and NOAA storm data, as of the date above. No addresses are published here. Any count under 5 is hidden and shown as "<5". Want your town's top homes? See the offer or the job board.
