How it works

No cold calls, no guessing. Every home on your list comes out of public records, and I look at each one before it gets a postcard.

  1. Records

    I pull your town's assessor and permit records and score every home for siding and roof age.

  2. Street View

    I check each top home on Street View and drop the ones already redone.

  3. Postcards

    Postcards go out to those homes. Homeowners who want an estimate fill out a short page.

  4. Your estimate

    You get notified and go do the estimate.

You only talk to homeowners who asked.
