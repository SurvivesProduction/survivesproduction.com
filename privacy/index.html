Privacy notice

This is a plain-language description of what Survives Production collects and why.

What I collect from the "2 free homes" form

Your name, your town and the best number to reach you. The form opens an email to me, so I also see your email address and anything else you write. I use it to send you the homes and to follow up. I don't sell it.

Where homeowner property data comes from

Property details (year built, siding and roof, assessed size) come from public town and state records. The Job Board shows homes without names, exact addresses, or tax and delinquency data.

Homeowner estimate requests

When a homeowner fills out an estimate request or calls from a postcard, what they share goes only to the one crew for that territory. It isn't shared with other crews and isn't sold.

Asking to be removed

If you're a homeowner who got a postcard, or a crew who filled out the form, email hello@survivesproduction.com and I'll remove you and stop mailing you.
