How it works

  1. Town records

    I pull your towns' public assessor and permit records and score every home for siding and roof age.

  2. Street View check

    I look at each top home on Street View and drop the ones already redone.

  3. Three postcards

    The homes that are left get a postcard sequence. Homeowners who want an estimate fill out a short page or call.

  4. You get a text

    When a homeowner asks for an estimate, you get a text. You go do the estimate.

You only talk to homeowners who asked.

          Questions

          What counts as an estimate request?

          What if a homeowner calls me directly?

          If the homeowner says the call came from the card, it counts. Tell me and I'll log it.

          What happens to my homes if I leave?

          You keep every estimate request you've received. Homes mailed for you stay off-limits to other crews for 12 months, then return to your territory's pool.

          Why one crew per territory?

          So a homeowner who raises a hand is sending that request to one crew, and you aren't racing another crew to the same mailbox.

          What happens to the 2 free homes if I don't sign up?

          They're retired and never mailed for anyone else.
